## Enforcement gaps

- **Startup & hygiene (`INV-001`, `INV-029`).** `main.py` skips Conda checks, `find_pandoc` runs lazily, and `.gitignore` / `manifest.sha256` lack pytest checks.
- **Prose-only tool prohibitions (`INV-018`, `INV-025`, `INV-030`, `INV-032`, `INV-033`; `ASM-011`, `ASM-035`).** `bash` and `web_search` remain globally bound: under Bypass (or approved Ask), `bash` can mutate files without `DocumentWorkspace`, citekey, or `@grill` guards; `web_search` is forbidden on Citation turns only in prose; raw author-year text bypasses `_unknown_new_citation_keys`.
- **Approval & file-scope edges (`INV-004`, `INV-009`, `INV-013`, `INV-018`, `INV-024`; `FAIL-025`, `ASM-010`, `ASM-032`).** Ask silently denies summaries `> 8,192` B (`FAIL-025`); Bypass auto-approves previewed MCP bindings and same-name `SkillInstaller` updates; direct UI RPCs (`document.save`, `bibliography.set_style`/`remove`/`export`) skip Ask; `read_file`/RAG reads are not confined to `<projectRoot>`; duplicate `tool_call_id`s are unchecked; `load_skill_runtime` has a hash-to-read TOCTOU window.
- **RAG store divergence (`INV-008`, `INV-012`; `FAIL-001`..`FAIL-003`).** `folder_meta.json`, Chroma, and `raw.json` have no cross-store transaction across prune, emptied-file re-ingest, or Stop.
- **Finalization, Stop drain & MCP bounds (`INV-005`, `INV-027`, `INV-031`; `ASM-013`, `ASM-031`).** Extended rewrite/config errors finalize as completed error text; `LocalWorkScope` has no hard drain deadline (CLI binds none); non-`web_search` MCP servers lack `get_tools()` timeouts.
- **Log privacy & UI details (`INV-021`, `INV-022`, `INV-028`; `FAIL-036`).** Exception tracebacks can log `ToolInvocationError` kwargs or provider bodies (`FAIL-036`), extended-thinking sub-calls bypass `TurnTimingTrace`, `.annotation-comment` uses fixed `13px`, and `Composer` omits `keyCode === 229`.
